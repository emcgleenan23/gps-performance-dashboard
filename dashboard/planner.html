    How the suggestions work

    Pitch sessions only; recovery and primer work is gym-based. Sessions are placed by days from the last match and to the next: ≈75% of a match's distance on MD-4, ≈50% on MD-2, ≈60% in a short week, nothing on MD+1, MD+2 or MD-1. A match is the selection's average per player-match (40 min or more). HSR, sprint and TAL start from the selection's training ratio to distance and can be set directly with the metric buttons. Weeks with no fixture get a match on the usual match day. Times are start times; the small figure beside each is hours since the last pitch session or match. ACWR = the week ÷ the mean of the previous four; EWMA ACWR after Williams et al. HSR and sprint guides (0.6–0.9× and 0.6–1.1× a match per week) follow Buchheit et al. (2023). Guides, not safety limits. Players are pseudonymised.
